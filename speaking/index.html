Plan A · 10-minute drill

10 minutes · focus: Fluency under time pressure

A short, repeatable session for weekdays. It trains the two skills that decide most CAE Speaking marks: starting quickly, and keeping going for a full two minutes without drying up.

  1. 1Warm-up · Interview2 min

    Answer each question out loud in two or three sentences. Do not prepare — the point is to get your mouth moving in English before the timed task.

    • › Where are you from, and what do you like most about it?
    • › What kind of work or study are you doing at the moment?
    • › How do you usually spend your weekends?
  2. 2Part 2 · 1 minute preparation1 min

    Look at Image A and Image B. You have one minute to make notes — three or four words per idea is plenty. Do not write sentences.

    • › Note one similarity and two differences.
    • › Note one advantage of each.
    • › Decide now which one you will say you prefer, and why.
  3. 3Part 2 · Long turn4 min

    Speak for two minutes without stopping. Structure: introduce what the pictures show → compare them → say which you prefer and why → finish with a short conclusion. Then repeat the whole turn a second time, trying to be more precise.

    • › Both of these photographs show …
    • › The most obvious difference is that …
    • › What strikes me about the second one is …
    • › On balance, I'd say … because …
  4. 4Review · Self-assessment3 min

    Listen back to your recording, or simply recall what you said. Write down two things that went well and one thing to fix next time. Then submit your scores below.

    • › Did I fill the full two minutes?
    • › Did I use any advanced vocabulary, or only basic words?
    • › How many times did I stop to think?
